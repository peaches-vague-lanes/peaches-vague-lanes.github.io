Privacy policy

This app is a private Home Assistant installation used by a single household. It is not offered to the public.

  • Signing in with Google lets Home Assistant read and control the household's own Google Nest devices.
  • Device data stays on the household's own Home Assistant server. It is not sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party connections).
